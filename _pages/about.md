Welcome to my personal website!

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [*Agricultural Economics and Policy Group*](https://aecp.ethz.ch/) at ETH Zurich since 2022. My research and teaching focus on examining the economic decisions of farms and their outcomes under environmental constraints, market incentives, and climate risk and uncertainty.

My current research and teaching focus on two main areas:

 (1) whether and how climate change affects farmers systemically, how they respond to these changes, and how their responses shape economic and environmental performance

  (2) how the transition to biodiversity-friendly agricultural systems influences economic viability and resilience at both the farm and supply-chain levels.

**I am on the 2025–2026 academic job market and welcome interview invitations.**
